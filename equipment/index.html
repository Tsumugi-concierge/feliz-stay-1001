Equipment

Sofa Bed

Pull the lower part of the sofa toward you to open it into a bed.

Air Conditioner

Press AUTO, COOLING, or HEATING to turn on. Adjust temperature and fan volume with the +/- buttons.

TV

Use the remote to power on/off and navigate Smart TV menus.

Hot Water

Press ON/OFF on the water heater controller before using hot water.

Cooking Stove

Push and turn the knob to ignite. Hold until stable. Turn fully back to switch off. Always check gas is off.

Microwave

Place your plate inside, choose wattage and time, then press START.

Toilet Washlet

Press REAR or BIDET to activate the spray. Adjust with Weaker / Stronger buttons.
